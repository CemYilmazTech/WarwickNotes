# Warwick Notes

A collection of lecture notes from Year 1 and Year 2 at the University of Warwick.

## Disclaimer

Some notes may be incomplete or unfinished — they were written during lectures and may have gaps. Use them as a supplement, not a replacement for official materials.

## Contributing

Feel free to contribute! If you'd like to add notes, fix errors, or fill in gaps:

1. Fork the repo
2. Make your changes
3. Open a pull request

All contributions are welcome.
